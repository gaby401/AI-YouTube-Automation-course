# Module 5 — Voice, Music, Captions & Assets

[← Module 4](04-ai-video-generation.md) | [Course Home](../README.md) | [Next: Module 6 →](06-automated-workflows.md)

## Goal

Create a recognizable audio-visual style that can be repeated across videos without rebuilding every decision from scratch.

## 1. Voice Options

Choose one primary narration approach:

- Your own recorded voice
- A hired voice actor
- A licensed AI-generated voice
- On-camera speech with light cleanup

Whatever you choose, consistency matters. Viewers should not feel like every upload belongs to a different channel.

## 2. Build a Voice Standard

Document:

```text
VOICE STYLE:
PACE:
ENERGY:
PRONUNCIATION NOTES:
WORDS TO AVOID:
BRAND PHRASES:
PAUSE STYLE:
CTA STYLE:
```

If using synthetic voice, always confirm the provider permits your intended use and avoid imitating real people without permission.

## 3. Music Should Support, Not Fight

Music can add energy, emotion, and rhythm, but it should not make speech harder to understand.

Create rules for:

- When music is used
- Music volume under narration
- Intro/outro treatment
- Whether Shorts use music at all
- Which libraries or licensed sources are approved

Keep records of music licenses and usage rights.

## 4. Captions Are Part of the Edit

Captions should improve comprehension rather than cover the entire screen.

Review:

- Spelling
- Names
- Numbers
- Brand terms
- Timing
- Line breaks
- Placement
- Safe areas for platform UI

Do not assume automatic transcription is perfect.

## 5. Create Caption Presets

Standardize:

```text
FONT:
MAX WORDS PER LINE:
POSITION:
HIGHLIGHT STYLE:
PUNCTUATION STYLE:
EMOJI POLICY:
BRAND COLOR POLICY:
```

A preset makes future editing faster and keeps the channel recognizable.

## 6. B-Roll and Supporting Visuals

B-roll should answer one of these questions:

- What does this look like?
- Where is this happening?
- What product or interface is being discussed?
- Can I show proof?
- Can I demonstrate the result?
- Can I make an abstract idea concrete?

Avoid filling every gap with unrelated stock footage.

## 7. Reusable Asset Library

Create a small approved library containing:

- Logo versions
- Lower thirds
- Subscribe/follow animations
- CTA cards
- Transition templates
- Sound effects
- Music tracks
- Backgrounds
- Screenshots and product assets you are permitted to use
- Common B-roll

Store licensing information beside the asset whenever possible.

## 9. Create a Brand Sheet

Your brand sheet can be one Markdown file.

Example:

```text
CHANNEL NAME:
AUDIENCE:
VOICE:
PRIMARY FONT:
SECONDARY FONT:
CAPTION STYLE:
THUMBNAIL STYLE:
B-ROLL STYLE:
MUSIC STYLE:
INTRO RULE:
OUTRO RULE:
CTA RULE:
DO NOT USE:
```

This document becomes extremely useful when you later outsource editing.

## Module Assignment

Create your first channel brand sheet and apply it to:

- One long-form or source video
- Three short-form clips
- One reusable caption preset
- One reusable CTA treatment

## Completion Check

You are ready for Module 6 when another editor could look at your brand sheet and make a video that still feels like it belongs to your channel.

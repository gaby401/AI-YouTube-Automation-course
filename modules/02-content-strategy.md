# Module 2 — Niche, Audience & Content Strategy

[← Module 1](01-foundations.md) | [Course Home](../README.md) | [Next: Module 3 →](03-ai-scriptwriting.md)

## Goal

Build a content strategy that gives your automation system clear direction before you start generating scripts and videos.

## 1. Pick a Clear Audience

Avoid creating content for "everyone." A useful audience statement looks like this:

> I create content for **[specific person]** who wants **[specific outcome]** without **[specific frustration]**.

Examples:

- Beginner creators who want to grow a faceless YouTube channel without learning advanced editing.
- Small business owners who want to turn one weekly video into daily short-form content.
- Fitness beginners who want simple home workouts without expensive equipment.

## 2. Choose a Niche With Enough Depth

A strong niche should give you enough material for dozens or hundreds of videos.

Score potential niches from 1–5 on:

- Personal knowledge or interest
- Audience demand
- Number of repeatable topics
- Monetization potential
- Ease of producing visual content
- Ability to create a unique angle

Do not select a niche only because someone claims it has a high CPM. Sustainable output matters more.

## 3. Build Content Pillars

Content pillars prevent random posting.

Choose 3–5 categories you can repeatedly create around.

Example for an AI creator channel:

1. AI tool tutorials
2. Automation workflows
3. Content strategy
4. Case studies
5. Creator monetization

Every future video should fit at least one pillar.

## 4. Create Repeatable Formats

A format is more useful than a single idea because it can generate many videos.

Examples:

- "3 mistakes beginners make with ___"
- "I tested ___ for 7 days"
- "Before you buy ___, know this"
- "How to do ___ in 5 minutes"
- "The fastest way to ___"
- "___ vs ___"
- "I automated ___ — here's what happened"

Formats make scripting, editing, thumbnails, and publishing easier to template later.

## 5. Build an Idea Bank

Create a simple idea database with these fields:

```text
IDEA
CONTENT PILLAR
FORMAT
TARGET VIEWER
PROBLEM / DESIRE
HOOK
PLATFORM
STATUS
RESULT
```

Your first goal is 30 ideas, not 300.

## 6. Use the Content Triangle

Before producing an idea, check three things:

### Relevance
Does your target viewer care?

### Packaging
Can the idea be expressed with a strong title, hook, or visual?

### Delivery
Can the actual video satisfy the promise?

If one side is weak, the video becomes harder to grow.

## 7. Plan for Repurposing Before Recording

If you want to create short-form clips from a longer source video, structure the source content so individual sections can stand on their own.

Good source videos include:

- Clear topic changes
- Strong statements
- Short stories
- Numbered points
- Questions followed by concise answers
- Useful demonstrations
- Standalone lessons

That makes AI-assisted clipping more effective later.

### Practical exercise

Record a 5–15 minute source video containing at least five standalone moments. Then test how well those moments can be extracted into short clips.

## 8. Create a 10-Video Starter Plan

Before moving on, plan your first ten videos.

Use this mix:

- 4 evergreen educational videos
- 2 comparison or opinion videos
- 2 problem/solution videos
- 1 experiment or case study
- 1 broader discovery-focused topic

For each, write:

```text
WORKING TITLE:
TARGET VIEWER:
CONTENT PILLAR:
FORMAT:
CORE PROMISE:
PRIMARY PLATFORM:
REPURPOSING POTENTIAL:
```

## Completion Check

You are ready for Module 3 when you can look at any new idea and quickly determine:

- Who it is for
- Why they would care
- Which format it belongs to
- How it could be packaged
- Whether it can be repurposed

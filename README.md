## 🎬 Start Here

This free course teaches you how to design a practical content automation workflow using AI tools for research, scripting, editing, repurposing, publishing, optimization, and scaling.

You do **not** need to be a programmer. The goal is to understand the workflow first, then automate the repetitive parts step by step.

### What you will build

By the end of the course, you will have a repeatable system that can help you:

- Find content ideas faster
- Turn ideas into scripts with AI
- Produce videos with less manual editing
- Repurpose long-form videos into Shorts, TikToks, and Reels
- Build reusable prompts and templates
- Create a publishing workflow
- Track performance and improve future videos
- Monetize a channel responsibly
- Scale from one-off videos into a content engine

# 🧠 Course Philosophy

This course is about **automation with judgment**, not mass-producing low-quality spam.

Good automation should remove repetitive work while keeping the important human decisions:

- Is the idea worth publishing?
- Is the information accurate?
- Is the opening strong enough?
- Does the video deliver what the title promised?
- Does the edit feel natural?
- Is the content actually useful or entertaining?

The best system is not the one with the most AI tools. It is the one that lets you consistently publish better content with less wasted effort.

---

# ✅ Recommended Learning Path

1. Complete Modules 1–3 before buying or subscribing to lots of tools.
2. Create one complete video manually with AI assistance.
3. Identify the parts that consume the most time.
4. Automate those parts first.
5. Create templates for anything you repeat.
6. Repurpose strong source content into multiple platform-specific clips.
7. Test a faceless automated series only after you understand what good content looks like.
8. Review analytics every week and feed what you learn back into your workflow.

## 🎯 First Assignment

Choose **one niche**, **one target viewer**, and **one primary platform**.

Write down:

- Who is this content for?
- What problem, desire, or curiosity does it serve?
- What type of video will you make repeatedly?
- What would make someone stop scrolling?
- What outcome do you want after your first 10 videos?

Then continue to **[Module 1: Foundations of Content Automation →](modules/01-foundations.md)**

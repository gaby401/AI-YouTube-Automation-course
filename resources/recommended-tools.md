## Start With the Bottleneck

Do not build a giant stack first. Choose a tool because it removes a specific problem.

## Long-Form → Short-Form Repurposing

### ⭐ OpusClip — Featured Tool

Best fit: creators who already have source videos and want AI-assisted short-form clip discovery, captions, reframing, and a broader repurposing workflow.

👉 **[Try OpusClip](https://opus.pro/?via=b4eee1)**

Official site: https://www.opus.pro/

## Transcript-Based Editing

### Descript

Best fit: dialogue-heavy videos, podcasts, tutorials, and creators who prefer editing through text.

Useful capabilities currently include transcript-based editing, filler-word removal, captions, audio cleanup, clip creation, and recording tools.

Official site: https://www.descript.com/

## General Social Video Editing

### CapCut

Best fit: creators who want broad manual editing controls plus templates and AI-assisted social-video tools.

Its current long-to-short workflow includes automatic clip generation, captions, smart cropping, resizing, and subject tracking.

Official site: https://www.capcut.com/

## Browser-Based AI Video Creation

### VEED

Best fit: creators who want browser-based editing, natural-language editing commands, captions, resizing, and AI generation workflows.

Official site: https://www.veed.io/

## Remote Recording + Repurposing

### Riverside

Best fit: interviews, podcasts, remote guests, and creators who want high-quality recording plus transcript editing and AI clip generation.

Official site: https://riverside.fm/

## Simple Stack Examples

### Beginner talking-head creator

```text
AI assistant → Camera / phone → Editor → OpusClip → YouTube / TikTok / Reels
```

### Podcast creator

```text
Riverside → Master edit → OpusClip or Magic Clips → Social platforms
```

### Faceless educational creator

```text
AI research assistant → Script → Voice → Visual assets → Editor → Repurposing tool → Platforms
```

### Transcript-heavy creator

```text
Record → Descript → Final source video → OpusClip → Platform variants
```

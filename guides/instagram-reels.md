# Instagram Reels Strategy Guide

[← Course Home](../README.md)

## Goal

Create Reels that are visually clear, easy to understand without sound, and reusable inside a broader content engine.

## Build Around a Clear Promise

Before editing, write:

```text
WHO IS THIS FOR:
WHAT THEY WILL GET:
FIRST-FRAME IDEA:
CORE POINT:
CTA:
```

## Make the First Frame Useful

The first frame can communicate through:

- On-screen text
- A result
- A product or demonstration
- A facial reaction
- A before/after state
- A direct question

Do not spend the opening on branding animations.

## Caption for Silent Viewing

Many viewers encounter short-form video with limited or no audio.

Check:

- Caption accuracy
- Readability
- Line breaks
- Placement
- Names and numbers
- Contrast against the background

## Use Covers Deliberately

Create a cover that helps someone understand the Reel when viewing your profile grid.

Keep it:

- Simple
- Legible at small size
- Consistent with your brand
- Focused on one idea

## Repurpose, Then Adapt

A master short can become a Reel, but review the CTA, cover, caption, music, and crop before posting.

## Reels Content Mix

Useful repeatable categories:

- Mini tutorials
- Behind-the-scenes
- Before/after
- Mistakes
- Quick lists
- Stories
- Product demonstrations
- Answers to audience questions

## Simple Testing Log

```text
TOPIC:
HOOK:
FORMAT:
DURATION:
COVER:
CTA:
RESULT:
SHARES / SAVES:
LESSON:
```

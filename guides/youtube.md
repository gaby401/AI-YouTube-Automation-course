# YouTube Strategy Guide

[← Course Home](../README.md)

## Goal

Build a YouTube system that connects topic selection, packaging, retention, repurposing, and analytics.

## The YouTube Content Loop

```text
IDEA
  ↓
TITLE + THUMBNAIL CONCEPT
  ↓
SCRIPT / RECORD
  ↓
EDIT
  ↓
PUBLISH
  ↓
ANALYTICS
  ↓
NEXT IDEA
```

## Before You Produce

Write these first:

```text
TARGET VIEWER:
CORE PROMISE:
WORKING TITLE:
THUMBNAIL CONCEPT:
OPENING HOOK:
WHY THIS VIDEO NOW:
```

If those are weak, do not hide the problem under more editing.

## Long-Form Structure

A practical structure:

1. Open with the promise.
2. Establish why the topic matters.
3. Deliver value quickly.
4. Use examples and demonstrations.
5. Remove repeated explanations.
6. End with a logical next action.

## Packaging Checklist

- Can the title be understood instantly?
- Does the thumbnail add information rather than repeat the title?
- Is the promise specific?
- Does the actual video satisfy the promise?
- Is the idea relevant to your target viewer?

## Repurpose Long-Form Into Shorts

Look for standalone moments inside long videos:

- Strong opinions
- Mistakes
- Quick tutorials
- Surprising facts
- Before/after examples
- Stories with a clear payoff
- Demonstrations

## Build Playlists Around Viewer Intent

Instead of random playlists, group videos around outcomes:

- Beginner start-here series
- Tool tutorials
- Case studies
- Advanced workflows
- Comparisons

## Weekly Review

Track:

```text
TOP VIDEOS:
WEAKEST VIDEOS:
BEST TOPICS:
BEST PACKAGING:
RETENTION PROBLEMS:
COMMENTS / QUESTIONS:
SUBSCRIBERS GAINED:
NEXT TEST:
```

## 30-Video Rule

Avoid rebuilding your entire strategy after every upload. Produce enough content to see patterns, then change one major variable at a time.

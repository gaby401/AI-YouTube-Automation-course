# Faceless Channel Guide

[← Course Home](../README.md)

## Goal

Build faceless videos that still feel intentional, original, and useful rather than looking like generic automated content.

## Choose a Faceless Format

Common formats:

- Narration + B-roll
- Screen-recorded tutorials
- Animated explainers
- Data / chart videos
- Documentary-style videos
- Image-led storytelling
- Product walkthroughs
- Text-led short-form videos

## Build Around Original Value

A faceless channel still needs a reason to exist.

Your originality can come from:

- Better research
- Better explanations
- Unique examples
- Original commentary
- Original experiments
- Better visualization
- A strong niche perspective

Automation should support that value, not replace it.

## Voice

Options include your own voice, a hired narrator, or a properly licensed synthetic voice.

Avoid cloning or imitating real people without permission.

## Rights Matter

Do not assume clips, images, music, or screenshots found online are automatically reusable.

Keep records of:

- Asset source
- License
- Permission
- Attribution requirements

## Two Automation Paths

### Path A — Create manually, then repurpose

Use this when you want maximum control over research, scripting, narration, visuals, and editing, then turn a strong source video into additional short clips.

Even with a highly automated workflow, review videos before publication whenever possible. Check factual accuracy, pronunciation, visual quality, rights, and whether the finished video actually provides value.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the script genuinely useful?
- Are claims verified?
- Does every visual support the narration?
- Are assets licensed appropriately?
- Does the voice sound natural?
- Are captions accurate?
- Does the video have a recognizable style?
- Would a viewer choose this over a generic AI-generated alternative?

## Goal

See practical ways to combine AI assistance, templates, and human review without over-automating the creative process.

# Workflow 1 — Beginner YouTube Workflow

```text
IDEA BANK
  ↓
AI RESEARCH ASSISTANCE
  ↓
HUMAN ANGLE SELECTION
  ↓
AI SCRIPT DRAFT
  ↓
HUMAN EDIT + FACT CHECK
  ↓
RECORD SOURCE VIDEO
  ↓
EDIT
  ↓
PUBLISH
  ↓
ANALYTICS REVIEW
```

Best for creators who are still learning what works.

# Workflow 5 — Batch Production

```text
DAY 1: 10 ideas
DAY 2: 5 scripts
DAY 3: record 5 videos
DAY 4: edit source videos
DAY 5: repurpose + schedule
DAY 6: analytics + comments
DAY 7: rest / overflow / planning
```

The exact schedule is less important than batching similar tasks.

# Where Automation Is Safest

Good candidates:

- Folder creation
- Naming files
- Transcription
- First-pass captions
- Drafting metadata
- Reformatting aspect ratios
- Creating candidate clips
- Updating production status
- Summarizing analytics

# Where Human Approval Matters Most

- Topic selection
- Final hook
- Factual accuracy
- Copyright / licensing
- Sponsor claims
- Final clip context
- Thumbnail and title approval
- Publication approval

# Workflow Audit Template

```text
STEP:
CURRENT METHOD:
TIME REQUIRED:
ERROR RATE:
CREATIVE OR MECHANICAL:
CAN TEMPLATE IT?:
CAN AI ASSIST?:
CAN AUTOMATE?:
HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED?:
NEXT IMPROVEMENT:
```

Use this audit once per month. Automate the largest proven bottleneck rather than adding tools for their own sake.
